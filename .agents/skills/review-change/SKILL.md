---
name: review-change
description: "Review a change and verify readiness for delivery."
---

Inspect the diff and relevant paths. Check correctness, boundaries, security, accessibility, and tests as applicable. Run available checks. Report findings, missing evidence, and remaining risks.

For Phase 03, verify that the final diff contains no application prototypes or native changes. Review contrast over actual backdrops, theme/forced-color cascade, reduced motion/transparency, focus and semantic state, original-source fidelity, and asset licenses. Distinguish confirmed defects from audit hypotheses. Record what was actually tested; owner visual approval remains a separate gate.
